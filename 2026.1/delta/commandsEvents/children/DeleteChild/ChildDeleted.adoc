[[evnt-ChildDeleted]]
===== Child deleted
<<existing-nodes, Existing node>> <<ChildDeleted.deletedChild>>, and all its <<ChildDeleted.deletedDescendants>>{fn-org370}, have been deleted from <<ChildDeleted.parent>>'s <<ChildDeleted.containment>> at <<ChildDeleted.index>>.{fn-org286}
All other children inside <<ChildDeleted.parent>>'s <<ChildDeleted.containment>> with index > <<ChildDeleted.index>> have been moved to next lower index.

.Technical name
`ChildDeleted`

[horizontal]
.Parameters
[[ChildDeleted.deletedChild, `deletedChild`]]deletedChild:: <<nodeIdType>>
of the node that was deleted in <<ChildDeleted.containment>> of <<ChildDeleted.parent>> at <<ChildDeleted.index>>.

[[ChildDeleted.deletedDescendants, `deletedDescendants`]]deletedDescendants:: <<nodeIdType>>[]
List of all node ids that used to be descendants (including children and annotations) of <<ChildDeleted.deletedChild>>.
Does not include <<ChildDeleted.deletedChild>>.

[[ChildDeleted.parent, `parent`]]parent:: <<nodeIdType>>
of the node a child was deleted from.

[[ChildDeleted.containment, `containment`]]containment:: <<metaPointerType>>
<<{m3}.adoc#Containment, Containment>> <<ChildDeleted.deletedChild>> was deleted from in <<ChildDeleted.parent>>.

[[ChildDeleted.index, `index`]]index:: <<indexType>>
where <<ChildDeleted.deletedChild>> was deleted from in <<ChildDeleted.containment>> of <<ChildDeleted.parent>>.
