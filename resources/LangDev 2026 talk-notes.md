## Najberg-Milne

1. They take some action: **Use LionWeb for integrating – primarily, model-driven/based – tooling, and (ideally) contribute to the LionWeb development (in some way).**
2. Take them on a journey: **Let us take you through a number of use cases — among which: realized ones.**
3. Have a clear-cut conclusion: **Not only was LionWeb essential for realizing these use cases, it also turned out that the stuff we tought up and we thought would be useful, turned out to actually be useful.** (In other words: we didn’t suffer from Ivory Tower Syndrome.)
4. Get their attention: **Wouldn’t it be “nice” to synchronize large models in real-time and with hardly any bandwidth usage?**

* Beginning: 4
* Middle: 2
* End: 3
* Epilogue: 1


## Structure

1. Short intro: what is LionWeb, who are we? [3']
2. Status update: release 2026.1 spec., updates of implementations [7']
3. Showcase several use cases, on a use case-per-use case basis [15']
	* Which use cases?
	* Time per use case depends on importance of use case.
	* Per use case:
		1. What is it?
		2. What’s the value of using LionWeb?
		3. What did we learn from it?
4. Questions [5']
