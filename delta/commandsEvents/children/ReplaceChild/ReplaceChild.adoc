[[cmd-ReplaceChild]]
===== Replace child
Delete <<existing-nodes, current child>> <<ReplaceChild.replacedChild>>{fn-org371} inside <<ReplaceChild.parent>>'s <<ReplaceChild.containment>> at <<ReplaceChild.index>>, and all its descendants (including annotation instances).
Does NOT change references to any of the deleted nodes.{fn-org285}

Replace <<existing-nodes, existing node>> <<ReplaceChild.replacedChild>> inside <<ReplaceChild.parent>>'s <<ReplaceChild.containment>> at <<ReplaceChild.index>> with <<new-nodes, new>> or <<reused-nodes, reused>> node <<ReplaceChild.newChild>>.

See also <<splittableMessage>>.

.Parameters
[[ReplaceChild.newChild, `newChild`]]newChild:: <<singleChunkType>>
Subtree to form the new child that replaces <<ReplaceChild.replacedChild>>.
The _anchor node_ MUST contain the <<ReplaceChild.parent>> as _parent_.{fn-org357}
All nodes in that subtree MUST either be <<new-nodes>> or <<reused-nodes>>.

[[ReplaceChild.parent, `parent`]]parent:: <<targetNodeType>>
of the node we want to replace a child in.

[[ReplaceChild.containment, `containment`]]containment:: <<metaPointerType>>
<<{m3}.adoc#Containment, Containment>> we want to replace <<ReplaceChild.replacedChild>> in <<ReplaceChild.parent>>.

[[ReplaceChild.index, `index`]]index:: <<indexType>>
where we want to replace <<ReplaceChild.replacedChild>> at in <<ReplaceChild.containment>> of <<ReplaceChild.parent>>.

[[ReplaceChild.replacedChild, `replacedChild`]]replacedChild:: <<targetNodeType>>
