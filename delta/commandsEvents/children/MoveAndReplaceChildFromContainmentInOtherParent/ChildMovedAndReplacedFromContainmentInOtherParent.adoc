===== Child moved from containment in other parent and replaced existing child
<<existing-nodes, Existing node>> <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.movedChild>> (previously inside <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldParent>>'s <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldContainment>> at <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldIndex>>) has replaced the <<existing-nodes, existing>> <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.replacedChild>> inside <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.newParent>>'s <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.newContainment>> at <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.newIndex>>.
<<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.replacedChild>>, and all its <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.replacedDescendants>>{fn-org370}, have been deleted.

All other children inside <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldParent>>'s <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldContainment>> with index > <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.oldIndex>> have been moved to next lower index.

No other children inside <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.newParent>>'s <<ChildMovedAndReplacedFromContainmentInOtherParent.newContainment>> have been moved.
